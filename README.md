Kurdistan Smart Water AI 💧

An AI-powered system for predicting water shortages and optimizing water distribution in Kurdistan.

🎯 Project Goal

The main goal of this project is to use Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning to predict potential water shortages and help decision-makers improve water distribution.

📊 Data Sources

The system can analyze:

* Water supply
* Water consumption
* Rainfall
* Temperature
* Season
* Population
* Reservoir levels
* Water source data

🤖 AI Predictions

The system aims to predict:

* Areas at risk of water shortage
* Expected time of shortage
* Estimated water demand
* Expected shortage percentage

🚦 Warning System

🟢 Green: Normal water conditions

🟡 Yellow: Potential water shortage

🔴 Red: High risk of water shortage

💡 Example Prediction

District X may experience a 25% water shortage within the next 14 days.

🏥 Smart Recommendations

The system can recommend:

* Increasing water supply
* Optimizing water distribution
* Prioritizing hospitals and schools
* Sending early warnings
* Planning water resources before a crisis occurs

🛠️ Technologies

* Python
* Machine Learning
* Artificial Intelligence
* Pandas
* Scikit-learn
* Flask
* HTML / CSS / JavaScript

🌍 Vision

To support smarter and more proactive water management in Kurdistan by predicting water shortages before they become critical.
